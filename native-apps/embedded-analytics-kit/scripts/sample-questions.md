# SaaS Analytics Kit — Sample Questions

Complex questions that can be answered by the Cortex Agent using the Snowflake semantic view and Qlik dashboards together.

## Revenue & Growth

1. Which segment has the highest net revenue retention, and what's driving it — expansion or low churn?

2. Show me the MRR concentration risk — what percentage of total revenue comes from the top 3 accounts, and which regions are they in?

3. Compare expansion revenue as a percentage of base MRR across plan tiers. Which tier has the most upsell headroom?

4. If all currently churned accounts were still active at their last MRR, what would total ARR be today?

## Customer Health & Churn

5. Are there accounts with declining engagement (low logins, low API calls) but high MRR that might be churn risks?

6. What's the average login frequency for churned accounts vs active accounts in the 3 months before they left?

7. Which industry-segment combination has the worst churn rate, and how much revenue is at risk in similar active accounts?

8. Rank regions by revenue-weighted churn — not just account count, but by how much MRR was lost per region.

## Subscription & Pricing

9. What's the average time between signup and first expansion event for Enterprise accounts vs Mid-Market?

10. Show me accounts on the Starter plan with usage metrics (API calls, data scanned) above the Professional plan average — these are upgrade candidates.

11. What would total MRR be if every Professional account upgraded to Enterprise pricing? What's the gap between current and potential ARR?

## Cross-Dimensional

12. Give me a cohort analysis: group accounts by signup quarter and show how their MRR evolved over subsequent months.

13. Which region-industry pair generates the most expansion revenue relative to its base MRR? Where should sales focus?

14. Compare the APAC and EMEA portfolios: which has higher ARPA, better retention, and stronger engagement metrics?

15. Create a health score for each account combining MRR, expansion history, login frequency, and API usage. Which accounts score lowest?
